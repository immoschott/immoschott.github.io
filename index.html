About

I am a Principal Economist in the Division of International Finance at the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, where I work in the Global Modeling Studies section. My research studies how firms make decisions — on debt, investment, and hiring — and how those decisions shape business cycles, productivity, and the effects of monetary and tax policy. You can find my official Board homepage here.

Before joining the Federal Reserve Board, I was Associate Professor (with tenure) at the Université de Montréal. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the European University Institute. I have taught at Université de Montréal and Georgetown University.
